# Look Mechanics

- The penguin hood and hair stay consistent in shape and size.
- Use eye direction, slight head turn, and subtle hood/body lean for gaze.
- `000` and `180` should read mostly frontal with clear up/down intent.
- `090` and `270` should read through face turn and nose/pupil placement, not whole-sprite rotation.
- Keep the chest clip attached and only gently following the pose.
